About Us

Your Local Guide in Ait Bougmez

Welcome! I am El Houssine Boulkhoukh, a certified mountain guide with years of experience leading treks in the High Atlas. Based in the beautiful Ait Bougmez valley, we specialize in authentic mountain experiences.

Our team provides everything from summit climbs like M'goun to cultural walks through local Berber villages, ensuring safety and an unforgettable immersion into our way of life.

Popular Expeditions

Mount M'goun (4067m)

A multi-day trek to the second highest peak in Morocco. Incredible ridges and geological formations.

Ouzoud Waterfalls

A stunning day trip or overnight stay to the most famous waterfalls in North Africa.

Ait Ben Haddou

Step back in time at this UNESCO World Heritage Site, a masterpiece of clay architecture.
